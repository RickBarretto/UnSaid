# Product Strategy

Based on [ideas#24](https://github.com/RickBarretto/ideas/issues/24).

## Vision

Clean up spoken recordings quickly while preserving meaning, natural pacing,
and the original file. Work offline after model setup.

## Target User

End users who wants to edit recorded videos, removing pauses, hesitations, and recording noises before sharing it.

This user cares about simplicity, workflow efficiency and privacy. AI could be used to cut silences, but he thinks he could enhance the final result manually.

## Positioning

For creators cleaning spoken video, UnSaid is an open-source desktop editor
combining reviewable suggestions with precise waveform and spectrogram editing.
Initial speech support: Brazilian Portuguese and English.

## North Star Metric

Recordings reviewed and successfully exported per active creator per week.
Proposed measure; no telemetry is implemented or required.

## ICP

Solo educators, presenters, interviewers, and podcasters on Windows, Linux, or
macOS. They need local processing and spend too much time making repetitive cuts.

## Scope

Import → inspect or analyze → review → edit → preview → export.
Automation suggests; the creator decides. Start with reliable manual editing.
Multitrack composition, visual effects, color grading, motion graphics,
stylized captions, and social publishing are outside the initial scope.
